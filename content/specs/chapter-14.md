## Sections
**Overview** — The unified life: one identity, one mission, several arenas — versus the
compartmented life where work-self and home-self fight. "Balance" gets a careful veto.

**01 — Balance is a lie (and what it really means)** — The trainings' slogan, decoded: as a
strategy claim it means depth beats hedging — half-commitment to many visions produces
nothing (preview of Ch 27's depth principle). As a life philosophy it's a values judgment,
not a law. What IS true: you can't hold two competing visions at full engagement —
single-mindedness is how deep work and deep relationships get built.

**02 — Floors are not negotiable** — The antidote to "balance is a lie" being over-read:
floors. Sleep (Dawson & Reid honesty: sustained wakefulness degrades cognition; ordinary
slightly-short nights are not the same — but the protective floor stands), movement, one
relationship hour. Floors are the substrate that makes any vision possible — the exception
to single-mindedness, not a second vision.

**03 — The integration principle** — Compartmentalization spends energy on walls. Integration:
the mission serves the family; the family anchors the mission. Identify 2–3 arenas (work,
body, people) and the single thread of identity running through them (Ch 8: identity
stack's verbs work in every arena).

**04 — Scheduling the unified life** — The Perfect Week protocol as the reconciliation: 80%
depth on the mission, floors protected, 20% deliberately unstructured. The week is the unit
of integration (preview of Ch 27).

**05 — When floors get raided** — The temptation to let the mission eat the floors in the
name of depth; warning signs (sleep first, then people, then health, then output collapses
— then so does the mission). Steelman: genuinely urgent bursts exist; floors flex but never
zero.

**Key Ideas / Apply Today** — Key ideas: depth beats hedging in one vision; floors are
substrate; integrate don't compartmentalize; the week is the unit; floors flex, never zero.
Apply: list your arenas and the thread through them; guard one relationship hour; build 80/20
week; flag one raided floor to restore.
