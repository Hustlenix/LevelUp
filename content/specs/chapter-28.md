## Sections
**Overview** — The capstone: a complete one-person business in one day — not finished, but
assembled: skill + problem + channel, with an 8-hour build plan. The corpus's whole
argument converges here: belief aimed (Ch 1–5), identity built (Ch 8), consistency
banked (Ch 23), systems subtracted (Ch 26), week executed (Ch 27).

**01 — The formula** — Skill + problem + channel: your marketable skill meets a specific
group's specific problem, delivered through one channel (posts, videos, emails).
Non-negotiable: skill (Ch 22's one-hour law), problem (specificity — Ch 4's ending
definition), channel (one — Ch 11's tuning).

**02 — The day-one plan (8 hours)** — Hour 1–2: define the customer + their exact problem
in writing. Hour 3–4: define the offer (what it IS, what problem it solves, price).
Hour 5–6: infrastructure — payment (Stripe or Gumroad-style), scheduling (Calendly-style),
one page (Notion or landing page). Hour 7: write the first 5 pieces of content/prospect
outreach. Hour 8: publish a simple call-to-action and put the offer live. (Tool names are
examples, not endorsements; Gumroad/Stripe/Calendly/Notion are mentioned in the training.)

**03 — Price anchors** — The training's anchors, presented as examples: $100/hr coaching,
$27–97 digital products, $500–2,000 services. Honest add-on: price = problem severity +
delivery cost + proof; start low-proof and raise with proof (the J-curve applies to price
too).

**04 — The Pat Flynn case, accurately** — He made ~$8,000 in his first *monetizing* month
(October 2008) — after ~2 years of unpaid blogging groundwork; the story is real and
documented, and the honest lesson is the groundwork, not the overnight (the corpus's most
famous "overnight" is actually a lag lesson, Ch 6).

**05 — Freedom is the product** — The one-person business's real output is optionality:
the compounding knowledge habit (Buffett — honest pages framing from Ch 22), the
consistent week (Ch 27), the non-desperate want (Ch 11). The empire isn't size; it's
independence. Close the book: 28 chapters, one engine — belief aimed, action bridged,
weeks compounded.

**Key Ideas / Apply Today** — Key ideas: formula; 8-hour day one; price anchors as
examples; Pat Flynn honestly; freedom = optionality. Apply: run the 8-hour plan this
weekend; write the one-page offer; publish the first CTA; schedule month-one's weekly
units; reread Chapter 6 when month two feels flat.
