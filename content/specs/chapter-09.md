## Sections
**Overview** — "Fake it till you become it," repaired: you borrow states you can't yet access
from within, then repay the loan with repetition. The body is the fastest route in.

**01 — What a borrowed state is** — States (confidence, calm, urgency) are usually treated as
weather — either present or not. The trainings' claim: states are switchable via the stack's
lower floors. When your own state-library lacks the entry, borrow: mimic posture, breath,
pace, phrasing of someone who embodies it.

**02 — The body enters first** — Posture claims: original power-posing results (hormone
changes, risk behavior) did NOT replicate; what survived is felt power — brief, self-reported,
real enough to change the next five seconds. The honest version is not nothing; fiv
seconds is the size of a decision. Breath: slow exhale = first-line state tool.

**03 — Borrowing belief from your future self** — Combine with Chapter 3: "act from the
schedule of the version who already did it." Mental rehearsal of the borrowed role uses the
motor-imagery machinery (Ranganathan — imagery trains execution), so borrowing with rehearsal
= practice, not pretending.

**04 — The repayment plan** — Borrowed states become owned states through repetition + real
behavior: behavioral activation (act first, feel follows — Dimidjian). Borrow swallows are
fine; borrowed identity forever is impostorism. The loan is repaid by doing the bridge
behaviors (Ch 5).

**05 — When borrowing goes wrong** — Don't borrow from a fantasy person; borrow from a real
archive (someone you know, a documented case). And never use the borrowed state to skip
preparation — it buys you the start, not the outcome.

**Key Ideas / Apply Today** — Key ideas: state is switchable; body first; honest felt-power;
rehearse the role; repayment through repetition. Apply: choose one real model; enter
tomorrow's hard moment in their posture and pace; rehearse the first 30 seconds tonight;
log what the borrowed state let you start.
