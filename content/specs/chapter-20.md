## Sections
**Overview** — The modern devolution: novelty → flatness → escape. The phone is never the
first interruption; it's the default answer to every empty minute — and the emptiness is
where attention gets built.

**01 — The novelty curve** — The pattern: new stimulus (novelty spike) → plateau (flat) →
escape reflex (the swipe). Every plateau is an extinction event for attention — if you can't
sit in flatness, you can't sit in study, practice, or intimacy. Evidence hooks: minds wander
~47% of the time (Killingsworth & Gilbert) and trained attention is the alternative;
compulsive stimulation raises the base need (Lembke's homeostasis — the reputable dopamine
frame; no receptor-reset claims).

**02 — The escape reflex** — What the reflex really is: exposure intolerance dressed as
boredom. Waiting rooms, red lights, lulls — the reflex fires before the feeling forms.
Map your personal reflex inventory: which 10-second gaps go to the hand?

**03 — The tolerated boredom is a muscle** — Exposure-response prevention (Sepah's actual
mechanism: stimulus + response control for compulsive tech use): the urge rises, peaks,
habituates — IF you observe without acting. The Boredom Tolerance Ladder protocol (3-week
build) trains the muscle daily.

**04 — What boredom unlocks** — Single-task endurance (Ch 25), deep work (Ch 24), flow entry
(Ch 12 — flow needs unbroken attention), and creative incubation. "Boredom is the antechamber
of the superpower."

**05 — Design, not deprivation** — Ends with the honest reframe: this isn't monasticism; it's
renegotiating defaults. Keep the fun that's chosen; remove the consumption that's default.
One daily 30-minute stimulation-free block as the permanent floor.

**Key Ideas / Apply Today** — Key ideas: novelty→flat→escape; the reflex is exposure
intolerance; observe without acting; ladder protocol; chosen fun survives. Apply: run Week 1
of the ladder (pause + label before each trigger); one 30-min no-stimulation block daily;
delete one default app; log the habituation curve.
