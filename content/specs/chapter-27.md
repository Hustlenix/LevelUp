## Sections
**Overview** — The yearly plan dies in month one; the weekly plan compounds. Claim: one
well-executed week outperforms twelve months of intention. The unit of change is the week;
the ceiling is your thermostat.

**01 — The week is the unit** — Compounding logic: 52 wins beat one marathon. The weekly
review is the true planning horizon (Perfect Week synergy, Ch 14/24): Sunday design,
daily execution, weekly tollgate (Ch 4's tollgates at week scale). "One week vs 12 months"
is this book's arithmetic: 1.01^52 vs the grand reset.

**02 — The thermostat, honestly** — Hendricks' upper-limit problem as framework (grade B):
self-image regulates results like a thermostat — wins above the limit trigger
self-sabotage. Honest framing: the thermostat metaphor is useful, the mechanism is
psychology (self-worth, Ch 15's green line; deservingness). Raising it: slow expansion of
the 70%-scale target (Ch 8's stack), not a pep-talk reset.

**03 — Execution catalysts, not motivation** — Why weekly execution beats yearly
inspiration (Ch 19 family): 70% rule (act before readiness — Marine doctrine), floors,
comeback speed (Ch 23). The lag honesty: 12-month thinking misreads flat months as
failure (Ch 6's compression); weekly thinking reads them as progress.

**04 — The accountability structure** — The week's spine: one God-Mode block (Ch 21),
three flow blocks (Ch 12), admin windows (Ch 24), review Sunday. The week is small enough
to inspect (tollgate) and big enough to matter (initiative movement).

**05 — The 12-month horizon, repaired** — The yearly vision survives — as direction (Ch
4's backwards plan), not as schedule. Vision gives aim; weeks give the repetition;
results lag quietly (Ch 6). The union: 12-month aim, weekly unit, daily floor.

**Key Ideas / Apply Today** — Key ideas: 52 wins > 1 marathon; thermostat ceiling; 70%
execution; weekly tollgates; vision aims, weeks execute. Apply: run this week's design
(Sunday); pick the week's one initiative; execute at 70% scale; schedule the weekly
tollgate; raise one 70% target next month.
