## Sections
**Overview** — Decode "vibration" honestly: energy is not a mystical frequency; it's a
verifiable account — inputs (sleep, movement, breath, food, people) and drains (stimulation
exhaustion, negativity intake, fragmentation). Tone = your account's current balance.

**01 — The frequency claim, translated** — The trainings use "vibration" language; the
translation: baseline energy × mood tone × attention quality. What evidence supports: sleep
direction (Dawson & Reid: deprivation degrades cognition and self-control — honest <7h
caveat), movement, and the negativity bias (bad inputs genuinely outweigh equal good ones —
Baumeister direction, no fake ratios). "Raising your frequency" = funding the account and
trimming the drains.

**02 — The builders** — Ranked by leverage: sleep (floor), movement (fastest), breath
(minute-scale), protein timing (sustained), people (the mood multipliers — one good
conversation vs one doom scroll). Morning ritual hook: the first hour sets baseline tone —
Morning Calibration's role.

**03 — The drains** — Stimulation exhausts (dopamine-costing loops — Ch 20 preview), input
diet (news/comparison), fragmentation (interruptions: faster work, more stress — Mark).
Drains are subtractable — subtraction is the master skill (Ch 26 preview).

**04 — Tone engineering over the week** — You can't high-frequency a schedule that eats
itself: the Perfect Week protocol (floors + blocks + slack) is the weekly generator, and the
Boredom Tolerance Ladder restores the base consumption level. Daily: one builder before any
drain.

**05 — The honest ceiling** — Energy work does not replace skill or bridge-building; it
makes them cheaper and more consistent. "High frequency" is a means, not an outcome —
measure it by what you did, not how you felt.

**Key Ideas / Apply Today** — Key ideas: energy = account; builders vs drains; first hour sets
tone; subtraction beats addition; frequency is fuel, not finish line. Apply: fund one builder
before any screen; identify one drain to retire this week; run one no-stimulation block;
protect sleep as a meeting.
