## Sections
**Overview** — The ancient technique: nightly visualization in a deeply calm state to "rewire"
behavior. Distinguish the tradition (with its claims about theta and childhood programming) from
the modern evidence (motor imagery is real training).

**01 — What the tradition claims** — Theta waves, high suggestibility, "programming before age
7," hypnosis-like receptivity. Present faithfully, then check: Sabourin et al. found more theta
in highly hypnotizable people and more theta during hypnosis — direction supported; the
developmental claims ("age 7", "gateway") are beyond the evidence.

**02 — What the evidence actually shows** — Yue & Cole: four weeks of imagined pinky abduction →
22% strength gain vs 30% physical. Ranganathan et al. (Cleveland Clinic): 12 weeks → 35% mental
vs 53% physical. Motor imagery recruits much of the same cortex as execution. Scope honesty:
this is skill and strength training, not wealth attraction.

**03 — The rehearsal protocol** — The nightly ritual, built from the evidence: (1) settle into a
calm, low-stimulation state (breathing, dim light); (2) rehearse the PROCESS of tomorrow's hard
moment in first person — sights, posture, the exact movements; (3) rehearse the difficult
segment specifically (the hard sentence, the awkward call), not the applause; (4) close with the
step you'll take in the morning. Process > outcome (Pham & Taylor).

**04 — Why calm helps** — Focused, low-distraction rehearsal = deliberate practice's imagery
twin; the "ancient" insight (state matters) matches modern findings that attention is the
currency (Chapter 1). Include the honest caveat about hypnosis claims from 03.

**05 — Rehearsal is training, not attraction** — Clarify the chapter's boundary explicitly:
"Visualization makes you readier, not luckier." It shortens the gap between intention and
execution; it never replaces execution.

**Key Ideas / Apply Today** — Key ideas: imagery trains cortex; 35% vs 53% with honest scope;
process rehearsal; calm state; readier not luckier. Apply: tonight's state-settling; rehearse
tomorrow's one hard moment; all-in repetition for 21 days; log one "it worked" moment weekly.
