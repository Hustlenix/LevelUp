## Sections
**Overview** — Everyday life runs on two tracks: outcomes (results, rankings, revenue —
the orange line) and worth (value independent of results — the green line). Most suffering
comes from welding the two into one.

**01 — The orange line** — What it is: external, measurable, real. Why it exists: evolution
and markets grade outputs. Honest defense: orange matters — it pays the bills and signals
progress; this is not an anti-ambition chapter.

**02 — The green line** — What it is: worth not conditional on results — stable across bad
weeks. Why it feels fake at first: we've fused the lines, so untangling feels like
self-deception (the filter from Ch 1 protects the fusion).

**03 — The fusion and its cost** — When lines fuse, every orange dip reads as a green dip —
identity damage from normal variance. Evidence hooks: wandering minds and low mood
(Killingsworth & Gilbert: mind wandering in either time direction correlates with
unhappiness — rumination is the fusion engine); the housekeeper lesson inverted (your
interpretation of the day modulates its yield — Crum: interpretation is a real lever, use
it on worth, not just outputs).

**04 — Separating the lines, practically** — Operations: morning declaration of the green
line (worth is not up for revision today); orange reviews weekly, never at night; failure
reframed as data about the orange task, not the green self. Worth rituals: deliberate
non-output time (people, play) as proof the green line exists without production.

**05 — The strange payoff** — Paradox, supported by the corpus: separating the lines raises
the orange line — non-desperate action decides better (scarcity → worse judgment; Ch 11) and
takes risks (Ch 2's delusion protocol needs a self that survives failure).

**Key Ideas / Apply Today** — Key ideas: two lines; fusion is the disease; interpretation is
a real lever; green-rituals; separation raises orange. Apply: name one fusion moment today;
morning green-line declaration; move orange reviews to the week; schedule one non-output
green ritual; Equation Check one self-verdict.
